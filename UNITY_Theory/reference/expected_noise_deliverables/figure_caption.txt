Injection and recovery for the six UNITY eclipse-mapping targets, using one fixed white-noise realization per target. The purple curve is the injected temperature-dependent offset, delta = atan[tan(40 degrees)*(1500 K/Teq)^3]. Black symbols show the recovered median longitude from independent full-visit fits, with q16–q84 profile-grid intervals. Expected white noise was calculated with the official online PandExo service using J-normalized PHOENIX stellar spectra and the accepted NIRSpec detector settings. The noise per 60-second wall-time bin is 58.9, 29.6, 81.4, 51.8, 53.7 and 89.9 ppm for HAT-P-30b, KELT-7b, WASP-178b, HAT-P-1b, HAT-P-13b and WASP-12b, respectively. Open symbols indicate that the flexible map does not pass the predeclared detection threshold (map-minus-uniform delta BIC < -6). The curve is an illustrative scaling inspired by Showman et al. (2020), not an extracted GCM prediction. One realization does not establish recovery rates or interval coverage.
