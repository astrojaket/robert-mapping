We simulated eclipse-centred JWST/NIRSpec G395M/G395H visits for six hot Jupiters. We used fixed circular geometry, fixed depths, and ideal 60-second samples. We set each band depth from a photon-weighted blackbody over 2.9–5.1 µm, with brightness temperature at 1.1 times the equilibrium temperature. We set target-specific white noise from saved PandExo calculations (29.6–89.9 ppm per 60 seconds) and generated 100 independent draws. We fitted each draw over the full visit and the eclipse-only samples with the saved profile grid for longitude, width, latitude, and timing. We averaged the normalized approximate BIC-weighted profile distributions with equal weight per draw. This mixture includes profile-fit uncertainty and noise variation; it is not a full posterior or a combination of repeated visits. The injected relation was δ = atan[tan(40°)(1500 K / Teq)^3], a Showman-inspired illustration with a chosen normalization, not an exact GCM prediction. The full-visit recovered slope median was -32.3 degrees per 1000 K; the injected-relation slope was -30.9, and the recovered slope was negative in 100 of 100 draws. The map-detection flag was true in 600 of 600 full-visit fits and 594 of 600 eclipse-only fits. All 6 injected longitudes fell within the mixture's 1σ range. These are simulation results, not formal false-alarm probabilities.
