UNITY expected-noise recovery under the temperature-scaled injected relation. Black points show the median of the equal-weight mixture of each independent noise draw's normalized approximate BIC-weighted profile distribution; inner and outer bars show the central 1σ and 2σ ranges. The purple curve is the analytic injected relation. The finite 100-draw mixture includes conditional profile-fit uncertainty and noise variation; its 2σ tails are simulation estimates. It is not a full Bayesian posterior and does not describe combining multiple real visits. The simulation uses fixed geometry and depth, approximate blackbody depths, target-specific PandExo white noise only, profile fits rather than full posterior sampling, and an illustrative Showman-inspired normalization rather than exact GCM predictions.
