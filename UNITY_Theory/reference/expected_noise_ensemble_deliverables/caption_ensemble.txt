UNITY expected-noise recovery under the temperature-scaled injected relation. Black points show the median of 100 recovered profile medians per target; inner and outer bars show the central 1σ and 2σ empirical ranges across independent noise realizations. The purple curve is the analytic injected relation. These empirical noise-realization ranges are simulation estimates, not posterior intervals or standard errors of the mean; the 2σ tails are especially limited by the finite 100-draw ensemble. The simulation uses fixed geometry and depth, approximate blackbody depths, target-specific PandExo white noise only, profile fits rather than full posterior sampling, and an illustrative Showman-inspired normalization rather than exact GCM predictions.
